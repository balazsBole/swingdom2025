Register for Museum Tour

Our main party venue is a museum! And not just any museum, it is the museum where machines make music. From the biggest barrel organs to the smallest musical boxes, Museum Speelklok has an amazing collection of mechanical music machines. Therefore we have arranged a private tour for our participants on Friday between 4 and 5 p.m. where you will be guided through the sounds of centuries-old music machines.
Unfortunately we have only 40 spots and a registration is required, so please register here only if you are sure you can make it!
