About SwingDom

Welcome to the annual swing dance festival in Utrecht! SwingDom is organised by the local Lindy hop community, Swing In Utrecht. Starting out as a small local event, SwingDom has grown to become the biggest international swing festival in the Netherlands!

Utrecht

This medieval city is known for its canals and an overwhelming amount of bicycles. The greatest pride of all Utrechters is the Dom tower, also represented in the name of SwingDom.

Swing in Utrecht

We are an inclusive international community of people who share a passion for swing dancing. Around the year we dance and teach classes in Lindy hop, Charleston, Solo Jazz, Blues, Shag and Balboa.
